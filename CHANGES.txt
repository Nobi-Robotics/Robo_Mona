V5 - Rounded cat ears

Changes from V4:
- Rounded ear tips increased from 1.7 mm to 4.5 mm radius.
- Fuller curved sides and a gentle outward lean replace the sharp triangular profile.
- Shallow inner-ear bowls added to the front-facing side of each ear.
- Ear top lowered 3 mm to Z187; nominal assembled size 140 x 112 x 187 mm.
- Outer head shell, screen opening, neck mechanism and six tentacles retain V4 dimensions.

Use both new head shells together (05_head_front and 06_head_rear).
All seven remaining parts can be reused from V4. The full kit is also included.
